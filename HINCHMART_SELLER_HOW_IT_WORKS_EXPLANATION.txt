1. WHAT IS HINCHMART SELLER PORTAL?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The HinchMart Seller Portal is a dedicated web platform for B2B manufacturers, 
distributors, and suppliers to manage their entire B2B wholesale business:
  - Onboarding and Seller Profile management
  - Creating and organizing Categories, Subcategories, and Brands
  - Uploading product media directly to AWS S3 Cloud Storage
  - Adding B2B products with Bulk Pricing, MOQ, Stock, HSN, and GST
  - Tracking approval status from Admin
  - Managing live inventory, orders, and logistics


================================================================================
2. HOW IT WORKS (STEP-BY-STEP WORKFLOW)
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 1: SELLER LOGIN & ISOLATED WORKSPACE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. How Seller Accesses the Portal:
   - Seller logs in with their registered phone number / email / OTP.
   - The system authenticates the seller and assigns their unique `sellerId` (e.g., '9').
   - Every request made by this seller automatically includes their Auth Token 
     and `X-Seller-Id: 9` header.

2. How Seller Data Isolation Works:
   - The seller has their own private catalog workspace.
   - The seller ONLY sees categories, brands, and products created by or assigned 
     to their seller account.
   - They do not see other sellers' private data.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 2: 3-TIER CATALOG SETUP (CATEGORY -> SUBCATEGORY -> BRAND)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before adding products, the platform organizes items in a structured 3-tier hierarchy:

1. Step 1: Category Setup (Level 1)
   - Seller clicks "Add Category" (e.g., "Textile Colors & Dyes").
   - Seller enters Name, Slug, Description, and uploads a Banner Image.
   - System saves it to the backend (`POST /api/categories`).
   - If the category name already exists on the marketplace, the system automatically
     links the existing category so no duplicates are created.

2. Step 2: Subcategory Setup (Level 2)
   - Seller clicks "Add Subcategory" (e.g., "Pink Textile Colors").
   - Seller selects the parent category ("Textile Colors & Dyes").
   - System links the subcategory under that parent category (`POST /api/subcategories`).

3. Step 3: Brand Setup (Level 3)
   - Seller clicks "Add Brand" (e.g., "ColorCraft").
   - Seller links the Brand to the Category and Subcategory.
   - Seller uploads the Brand Logo.
   - System registers the brand on the backend (`POST /api/brands`).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 3: HOW IMAGE UPLOADING WORKS (AWS S3)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Seller selects or drags an image file (PNG, JPG, WEBP).
2. The frontend validates the file (max 5MB).
3. The image is sent directly to the live AWS S3 upload service:
   `POST /api/images/upload?type=products`
4. AWS S3 generates a permanent, secure HTTPS image URL:
   `https://hinchmart-storage-191481838776-ap-south-2-an.s3.ap-south-2.amazonaws.com/products/xxx.png`
5. This S3 image URL is automatically attached to the product listing.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 4: HOW ADDING A PRODUCT WORKS (SELLER FORM FLOW)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When a seller clicks "Add New Marketplace Product":

1. Select Hierarchy:
   - Selects Category (e.g., "Textile Colors & Dyes")
   - Subcategory dropdown automatically updates to show subcategories under that category.
   - Seller selects Subcategory (e.g., "Pink Textile Colors").
   - Brand dropdown updates -> Seller selects Brand (e.g., "ColorCraft").

2. Enter Product Identity:
   - Product Title: e.g. "ColorCraft Rose Pink Reactive Dye"
   - SEO URL Slug: auto-generated (e.g. "colorcraft-rose-pink-reactive-dye")
   - SKU Code: auto-generated or custom (e.g. "SKU-PINK-001")

3. Enter Commercial & B2B Wholesale Details:
   - Selling Price: e.g. Rs 850.00 per unit
   - MRP: auto-suggested or custom (e.g. Rs 977.00)
   - Unit of Measurement: e.g. "KG", "PIECE", "COIL", "BOX"
   - Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ): e.g. 5 (Wholesale requirement)
   - Stock Quantity: e.g. 100
   - HSN Code: e.g. "3204" (GST Tax Code)
   - GST Rate: e.g. 18% (Parsed as integer 18)
   - 24-Hour Express Logistics: checkbox toggle

4. Attach Images & Specifications:
   - Product image uploaded to S3 is attached as primary photo.
   - Technical description (grade, specifications, usage instructions).

5. Submit to Backend:
   - Form validates all fields and sends `POST /api/products`.
   - Backend creates the record with a unique database Product ID (e.g. 790).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 5: APPROVAL & MARKETPLACE PUBLISHING LIFECYCLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
How the product moves from Seller to Buyer Storefront:

  [1. SELLER CREATION]
       |
       v
  Seller submits product -> Saved to database with `status: 'PENDING'`.
       |
       v
  [2. SELLER PORTAL VIEW]
       |
       v
  Product appears in Seller's Dashboard under "Pending Approval Review".
  Seller can view, edit price, or adjust stock anytime.
       |
       v
  [3. ADMIN REVIEW & MODERATION]
       |
       v
  Admin opens Admin Portal -> Reviews product details, pricing, and image.
  Admin clicks "Approve".
       |
       v
  [4. LIVE MARKETPLACE STOREFRONT]
       |
       v
  Status becomes `APPROVED` and `active: true`.
  Product is now visible to all B2B buyers on HinchMart marketplace.
  Buyers can search, add to cart, and place bulk wholesale orders.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 6: INVENTORY, PRICING & ORDER MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Once products are in the system, the seller can:
  - Quick Stock Update: Adjust available inventory directly from the table.
  - Price Adjustment: Update standard price and bulk discount tiers.
  - Active/Inactive Toggle: Temporarily pause listings if out of stock.
  - Delete/Archive: Remove discontinued products from their active inventory.

================================================================================
Summary:
1. Seller creates Category -> Subcategory -> Brand -> Product.
2. Images upload directly to AWS S3.
3. Products are saved to DB with `PENDING` status.
4. Admin approves -> Product goes LIVE on buyer marketplace.
